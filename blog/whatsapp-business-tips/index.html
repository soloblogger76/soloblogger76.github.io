WhatsApp Business: 9 setup tips for lead-gen businesses

Most businesses use WhatsApp as a phone. These nine changes turn it into a sales channel without any automation spend.

Media Puppies · 4 August 2026

For most Indian businesses, WhatsApp is where a lead becomes a conversation and where it quietly dies. Before spending anything on automation, these nine changes are free and they compound.

1. Use a Business profile, not a personal one

Business profiles carry your category, address, hours, website and a description. A personal number with a photo of your dog is a trust gap you are choosing to have.

2. Write a greeting that answers the first question

Most automatic greetings say thanks for contacting us, our team will reply soon, which tells the customer nothing. Replace it with your price range, your service area and what happens next. Answer the question they were about to type.

3. Build quick replies for your ten most common answers

Price range, service areas, timelines, process, payment options, portfolio link. Your team retypes these thirty times a day. Quick replies turn each into two keystrokes and — more importantly — make the answers consistent.

4. Use labels as a pipeline

New, Contacted, Quoted, Won, Lost. That is a CRM for a small team, built into an app you already have. The point is not tidiness; it is that nobody forgets a lead sitting between stages.

5. Set an away message with a real time in it

We're closed, we'll reply tomorrow before 11am beats we'll get back to you soon. A specific promise you keep buys patience.

6. Put the catalogue in

Even for services. Packages with price ranges, photographed. It lets a customer browse without asking, and it qualifies them before they take your team's time.

7. Use a click-to-WhatsApp link everywhere

A wa.me link on the site, in the ad, in your email signature, on the invoice. Pre-fill the message with context so you know which page they came from — that context is worth more than it looks when you are reading a list of enquiries at 9pm.

8. Answer in the customer's language

If they wrote in Hinglish, reply in Hinglish. Formal English replies to a casual message create distance instantly, and distance costs conversions.

9. Know when you have outgrown the app

The free Business app has real limits: one device properly, no automation beyond greetings and away messages, no integration with your CRM, no routing between team members. When any of those start costing you leads, you need the WhatsApp Business API through a provider.

Speed beats polish. A rough reply in two minutes outperforms a perfect one in two hours, every time.

The one rule underneath all of it

Respond fast. Not because it is impressive, but because in a competitive category the first business to reply wins a disproportionate share regardless of who was cheaper. Everything above is in service of that.

When your team cannot hold that standard through evenings and Sundays, that is the moment automation earns its cost — and the sequence is here.
